ZOTERO: TIPOS DE ÍTEM, CAMPOS Y CREADORES
Referencia para rediseñar la base de datos de Biblioteca UDC (relación muchos a muchos tipo <-> campo).

Fuentes
  - Descripciones: https://www.zotero.org/support/kb/item_types_and_fields (fuente en GitHub:
    zotero/zotero-docs, content/kb/item_types_and_fields.md; "Last updated 2026-05-18").
  - Qué campos y creadores lleva cada tipo, su orden, sus campos base y sus etiquetas: esquema oficial
    zotero/zotero-schema, schema.json, versión 45. La página web NO trae esa relación; solo agrupa
    los campos por categorías.
  - Generado el 2026-11-23.

Leyenda
  nombreInterno "Etiqueta"   nombre del campo en el esquema/API y su etiqueta en inglés en la interfaz.
  -> base: X                 el campo es una variante del campo base X (ver §4). P. ej. university -> publisher.
  (propia)                   la página no describe ese elemento; la descripción la redactó Claude.
  [*no citable]              la página lo marca con asterisco: no se usa al generar citas.
  creador*                   rol principal del tipo (el que hace de "autor" en la cita).
  Las descripciones oficiales se dejan en inglés, tal como aparecen en la página.

Resumen
  - 40 tipos en el esquema: 37 con campos bibliográficos + attachment (adjunto suelto)
    + note y annotation (sin campos).
  - 121 campos distintos; 58 de ellos son variantes de 13 campos base.
  - 770 pares tipo-campo y 170 pares tipo-creador.
  - 37 tipos de creador.

==============================================================================================================
1. TIPOS DE ÍTEM
==============================================================================================================
Cada tipo incluye los campos comunes de §2.1 salvo los que se indican como ausentes, y los cuatro de archivo
(§2.2) solo si dice "Archivo: sí". Después vienen los campos propios del tipo, en el orden del formulario de Zotero.

Annotation  (annotation)
------------------------
  (propia) Anotación de un PDF/EPUB (resaltado, comentario). No es un ítem bibliográfico.
  Campos: ninguno.

Artwork  (artwork)
------------------
  A piece of artwork (e.g., an oil painting, photograph, or sculpture). Also use this item type for other
  types of images or visual items (e.g., scientific figures).
  Campos comunes: todos
  Archivo (archive, archiveLocation, libraryCatalog, callNumber): sí
  Creadores: artist*, contributor
  Campos propios (3):
    artworkMedium "Medium"  -> base: medium
        The type of artwork or figure or the medium it was created with (e.g., "Watercolor painting", "Wood
        sculpture", "X-ray Crystallograph", "Scatterplot").
    artworkSize "Artwork Size"
        The dimensions of the artwork or figure.
    eventPlace "Event Place"
        The geographic location of an event.

Attachment  (attachment)
------------------------
  A standalone attachment file (e.g., a PDF, JPEG, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, or ODT file). Standalone attachment files
  have limited functionality in Zotero (e.g., they cannot be properly searched or cited). Always attach files
  to proper Zotero items.
  Campos comunes: todos salvo abstractNote, date, shortTitle, language, DOI, citationKey, rights, extra
  Archivo (archive, archiveLocation, libraryCatalog, callNumber): no
  Creadores: ninguno
  Campos propios (0):

Audio Recording  (audioRecording)
---------------------------------
  Any form of audio recording, including music, spoken word, sound effects, archival recordings, or audio-
  based scientific figures.
  Campos comunes: todos
  Archivo (archive, archiveLocation, libraryCatalog, callNumber): sí
  Creadores: performer*, originalCreator, composer, wordsBy, translator, contributor
  Campos propios (8):
    audioRecordingFormat "Format"  -> base: medium
        The format of an audio or video recording (e.g. "DVD," "CD," "MP3," etc.).
    seriesTitle "Series Title"
        Title of a series of articles within one issue of a journal (e.g., a special section or "From the
        Cover"). "Section Title" would likely be a more appropriate label. For citation purposes, this field
        is currently equivalent to "Series" and is erroneously used instead of series in some item types
        (e.g., Audio Recording, Map).
    volume "Volume"
        The volume of a (typically multi-volume) publication an article or chapter is published in. For
        numeric volumes, enter only a number. If the volume should be cited with a volume title, include the
        title in this field (e.g,. "2: Organizational Psychology").
    numberOfVolumes "# of Volumes"
        The number of volumes in a collective work.
    label "Label"  -> base: publisher
        The recording label distributing an audio recording in a market.
    place "Place"
        The place of publication for an item.
    runningTime "Running Time"
        The running time of any type of recording. Enter with the unit (e.g., 120 mins).
    ISBN "ISBN"
        The International Standard Book Number of a book or other publication.

Bill  (bill)
------------
  A proposed piece of legislation.
  Campos comunes: todos
  Archivo (archive, archiveLocation, libraryCatalog, callNumber): no
  Creadores: sponsor*, cosponsor, contributor
  Campos propios (8):
    billNumber "Bill Number"  -> base: number
        The identification number assigned to a proposed piece of legislation.
    code "Code"
        The name of the code in which a bill or statute is published.
    codeVolume "Code Volume"  -> base: volume
        The volume number of the code containing a bill or statute.
    section "Section"
        The section of a bill or statute being cited.
    codePages "Code Pages"  -> base: pages
        The pages in the code volume containing a bill or statute.
    legislativeBody "Legislative Body"  -> base: authority
        The legislative body (parliament, senate, etc.) debating a bill, holding a hearing, or passing
        legislation. [Marcado "*": no se usa en citas.]
    session "Session"
        The session of a legislative body in which a bill was introduced, statute was passed, or hearing was
        held.
    history "History"
        Resources related to the procedural history of a bill or statute.

Blog Post  (blogPost)
---------------------
  An article or entry posted to a personal blog website. For online articles published as part of a larger
  online publication (e.g., NYT Blogs), using Magazine Article or Newspaper Article generally yields better
  results.
  Campos comunes: todos
  Archivo (archive, archiveLocation, libraryCatalog, callNumber): no
  Creadores: author*, translator, commenter, contributor
  Campos propios (3):
    blogTitle "Blog Title"  -> base: publicationTitle
        The title of the blog or website containing a post or specific web page.
    websiteType "Website Type"  -> base: type
        Rarely used. Describes the genre of a webpage such as "personal blog" or "intranet".
    ISSN "ISSN"
        The International Standard Serial Number of a periodical or series publication.

Book  (book)
------------
  A book or similar published item. For government documents, technical reports, manuals, etc., use Report
  instead. This item type can also be adapted to fit many types of unusual items.
  Campos comunes: todos
  Archivo (archive, archiveLocation, libraryCatalog, callNumber): sí
  Creadores: author*, contributor, editor, translator, seriesEditor
  Campos propios (14):
    series "Series"
        Name of a series that contains multiple publications or presentations (e.g., "Cambridge Studies in
        Comparative Politics", "Special issue on Advances in Personality Assessment").
    seriesNumber "Series Number"
        The number of an item in a series.
    volume "Volume"
        The volume of a (typically multi-volume) publication an article or chapter is published in. For
        numeric volumes, enter only a number. If the volume should be cited with a volume title, include the
        title in this field (e.g,. "2: Organizational Psychology").
    numberOfVolumes "# of Volumes"
        The number of volumes in a collective work.
    edition "Edition"
        The edition of a work. Where numeric, enter as simple Arabic numeral (i.e., "2" rather than "second").
    publisher "Publisher"
        The publisher of an item.
    place "Place"
        The place of publication for an item.
    originalDate "Original Date"
        The original date an item was published. Enter in ISO format (year-month-day).
    originalPublisher "Original Publisher"
        The publisher of the original version of an item (e.g., the untranslated version).
    originalPlace "Original Place"
        The geographic location of the publisher of the original version of an item (e.g., the untranslated
        version).
    format "Format"  -> base: medium
        (propia) Formato físico o digital del ítem (p. ej. "eBook", "PDF", "CSV").
    numPages "# of Pages"
        The number of pages in a book.
    ISBN "ISBN"
        The International Standard Book Number of a book or other publication.
    ISSN "ISSN"
        The International Standard Serial Number of a periodical or series publication.

Conference Paper  (conferencePaper)
-----------------------------------
  A paper presented at a conference and subsequently published in a formal conference proceedings publication
  (e.g., as a book, report, or issue of a journal). For conference papers that have not been published in a
  proceedings, use Presentation.
  Campos comunes: todos
  Archivo (archive, archiveLocation, libraryCatalog, callNumber): sí
  Creadores: author*, contributor, editor, translator, seriesEditor
  Campos propios (13):
    proceedingsTitle "Proceedings Title"  -> base: publicationTitle
        The title of the conference proceedings where a conference paper was published.
    conferenceName "Conference Name"
        The name of the conference or meeting where a conference paper or presentation was presented.
    publisher "Publisher"
        The publisher of an item.
    place "Place"
        For Presentations, the place where the meeting was held or the presentation was made. For Conference
        Papers (published in a conference proceedings), use this field for the place where the proceedings was
        published. If separate locations are needed for the publication place and the location of the
        conference, leave this field blank and add Event Place and Publisher Place fields to Extra (see Citing
        Fields from Extra below).
    eventPlace "Event Place"
        The geographic location of an event.
    volume "Volume"
        The volume of a (typically multi-volume) publication an article or chapter is published in. For
        numeric volumes, enter only a number. If the volume should be cited with a volume title, include the
        title in this field (e.g,. "2: Organizational Psychology").
    issue "Issue"
        The issue number of a periodical. Typically, issues start at 1 at the beginning of each year/volume.
        If a periodical has only a volume number and no issue number, leave this field blank.
    numberOfVolumes "# of Volumes"
        The number of volumes in a collective work.
    pages "Pages"
        The page range of an item that is part of a larger publication. Also to be used for locators or
        article numbers in e-journals and ebooks.
    series "Series"
        Name of a series that contains multiple publications or presentations (e.g., "Cambridge Studies in
        Comparative Politics", "Special issue on Advances in Personality Assessment").
    seriesNumber "Series Number"
        The number of an item in a series.
    ISBN "ISBN"
        The International Standard Book Number of a book or other publication.
    ISSN "ISSN"
        The International Standard Serial Number of a periodical or series publication.

2.1 Campos comunes (presentes en casi todos los tipos)
------------------------------------------------------
  Además, la página describe como generales "Item Type", "Creators", "Date Added" y "Date Modified":
  no son campos del formulario sino propiedades del ítem (tipo, lista de creadores y fechas automáticas).
  (Item Type)
      The broad type of item. Item types are generally determined based on how items should be cited. See
      descriptions of Zotero’s item types above. You can store more specific information about the type of
      item (e.g., a novel versus a biography book) in the Type, Format, or Extra fields or using tags.
  (Creators)
      The individuals or entities creating or associated with a work. An item can have multiple creators of
      numerous different kinds. See the list of creator types below.
  (Date Added)
      The date and time an item was added in Zotero. This field is filled automatically.
  (Date Modified)
      The date and time an item was last modified in Zotero. This field is filled and updated automatically.
  title "Title"  [35]
      The principal title of an item. Should be entered in sentence case.
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos salvo case, email, statute
  abstractNote "Abstract"  [37]
      Short description of the work
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos salvo attachment
  date "Date"  [34]
      Date of publication. Use "Accessed" for the date an electronic resource was accessed.
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos salvo attachment, case, patent, statute
  shortTitle "Short Title"  [37]
      Short form of the title, often without the subtitle. Used mostly in footnote styles for subsequent
      citations
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos salvo attachment
  language "Language"  [36]
      The language of publication for an item. We recommend storing these as two letter ISO language codes
      followed by two letter ISO country codes (e.g., en-US for American English, or de-DE for German).
      
      The Language field is used to determine whether English Title Casing rules should be applied when
      formatting item titles. If the stored item data is in English (e.g., for translated titles), enter en-US
      or en-UK, even if the item content is in another language.
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos salvo attachment, computerProgram
  url "URL"  [38]
      URL (web-address) where the full item was accessed. Do not use this field for links to catalog records
      (e.g., libraries or Google Books) or abstracts—these should be instead added as links.
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos
  accessDate "Accessed"  [38]
      Date an electronic resource was accessed. Typically filled automatically. You can enter "today,"
      "yesterday," and "tomorrow" to quickly enter the corresponding date.
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos
  DOI "DOI"  [37]
      The Digital Object Identifier of an item. For items other than journal articles, the DOI can be stored
      in Extra. See Citing Fields from Extra below.
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos salvo attachment
  citationKey "Citation Key"  [37]
      (propia) Clave de cita del ítem (p. ej. para BibTeX/Better BibTeX). Campo reciente; la página no lo
      describe.
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos salvo attachment
  rights "License"  [37]
      The copyright terms, license, or release status for an item. [La página lo llama "Rights*" (no citable);
      en el esquema actual la etiqueta es "License".]
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos salvo attachment
  extra "Extra"  [37]
      Free field for storing additional information. You can also store additional variables not included in
      an item’s fields that can be used when making citations and bibliographies . See Citing Fields from
      Extra below.
      Tipos: todos los que tienen campos salvo attachment

4. CAMPOS BASE (equivalencias entre tipos)
==============================================================================================================
Zotero define campos base: varios campos con nombre propio en un tipo son "el mismo" dato en otro.
Al cambiar el tipo de un ítem, el valor pasa de la variante al campo base y viceversa; también se usan para
buscar y para exportar a CSL. Formato: base "Etiqueta" <- tipo: variante.
  authority "Authority" (no se usa tal cual en ningún tipo)
      <- bill: legislativeBody
      <- case: court
      <- hearing: legislativeBody
      <- patent: issuingAuthority
      <- standard: organization
  date "Date" (también se usa tal cual en 34 tipos)
      <- case: dateDecided
      <- patent: issueDate
      <- statute: dateEnacted
  medium "Medium" (no se usa tal cual en ningún tipo)
      <- artwork: artworkMedium
      <- audioRecording: audioRecordingFormat
      <- book: format
      <- bookSection: format
      <- dataset: format
      <- film: videoRecordingFormat
      <- interview: interviewMedium
      <- podcast: audioFileType
      <- radioBroadcast: audioRecordingFormat
      <- tvBroadcast: videoRecordingFormat
      <- videoRecording: videoRecordingFormat
  number "Number" (también se usa tal cual en 2 tipos)
      <- bill: billNumber
      <- case: docketNumber

      <- patent: patentNumber

  place "Place" (también se usa tal cual en 27 tipos)

  publicationTitle "Publication" (también se usa tal cual en 3 tipos)
      <- blogPost: blogTitle

5. TIPOS Y CAMPOS CITABLES QUE ZOTERO NO TIENE (van en Extra)
==============================================================================================================
La página indica escribirlos en Extra, una línea por variable, como "Variable CSL: valor"
(p. ej. "Type: musical_score", "Original Date: 1886-04-01", "Director: Kubrick || Stanley").
Salvo el tipo y las fechas, lo escrito en Extra no sobrescribe campos propios del ítem. Las fechas van en ISO
(año-mes-día); los rangos, con barra. Los nombres en dos partes se separan con "||".

5.1 Tipos de ítem
-----------------
  Figure  (CSL: figure)
      A figure included in a scientific or academic work.
  Musical Score  (CSL: musical_score)
      The written score for a musical work.
  Pamphlet  (CSL: pamphlet)
      An informally-published work. Typically smaller and less technical than a Report.
  Book Review  (CSL: review-book)
      A review of a book. Enter these as Journal, Magazine, or Newspaper Article, depending on where they were
      published, by providing a "Reviewed Author" creator.
  Treaty  (CSL: treaty)
      A legal treaty between two nations.

5.2 Campos y creadores
----------------------
  Varios de esta lista ya existen como campos en el esquema 45 (PMID, PMCID, Status, Event Place, Original Date,
  Original Publisher, Original Publisher Place -> originalPlace, Original Author -> originalCreator), pero solo
  en algunos tipos; en los demás siguen yendo en Extra.
  PMID  (CSL: PMID)
      The PubMed identifier.
  PMCID  (CSL: PMCID)
      The PubMed Central identifier.
  Status  (CSL: Status)
      The publication status of an item (e.g., "forthcoming", "in press", "advance online publication").
  Submitted Date  (CSL: Submitted)
      The date an item was submitted for publication.
  Reviewed Title  (CSL: Reviewed Title)
      The title of a reviewed work.
  Chapter Number  (CSL: Chapter Number)
      The number of the chapter within a book.
  Archive Place  (CSL: Archive Place)
      The geographic location of an archive.
  Event Date  (CSL: Event Date)
      The date an event took place. Enter in ISO format (year-month-day).
  Event Place  (CSL: Event Place)
      The geographic location of an event.
  Original Date  (CSL: Original Date)
      The original date an item was published. Enter in ISO format (year-month-day).
  Original Title  (CSL: Original Title)
      The original title of a work (e.g., the untranslated title).
  Original Publisher  (CSL: Original Publisher)
      The publisher of the original version of an item (e.g., the untranslated version).
  Original Publisher Place  (CSL: Original Publisher Place)
      The geographic location of the publisher of the original version of an item (e.g., the untranslated
      version).
  Original Author  (CSL: Original Author)
      A type of Creator. The original creator of a work.
  Director  (CSL: Director)
      A type of Creator. The director of a film, recording, or broadcast. In Zotero, "Director" is mapped to
      CSL author. If you need special labels for directors—"(Dir.)", enter the Director label in Extra.
  Editorial Director  (CSL: Editorial Director)
      A type of Creator. The managing editor of a publication ("Directeur de la Publication" in French).
  Illustrator  (CSL: Illustrator)
      A type of Creator. The illustrator of a work.

6. NOTAS PARA EL REDISEÑO DE LA BASE DE DATOS
==============================================================================================================
  - Relación muchos a muchos: item_type_fields(item_type, field, orden) y item_type_creators(item_type,
    creator_type, es_principal). El orden importa: es el del formulario.
  - Los campos base (§4) sugieren guardar el valor bajo el campo base y la variante como etiqueta por tipo
    (así, al cambiar de tipo, no se pierde nada).
  - Un mismo campo puede tener significado distinto según el tipo (type, place, section, history,
    versionNumber, committee, number, genre, institution): conviene que la descripción/ayuda viva en la
    relación tipo-campo y no solo en el campo.
  - Las partituras no tienen tipo propio en Zotero: la página propone usar otro tipo y "Type: musical_score"
    en Extra. El PoC ya usa document + "Tipo: partitura" en extra; habrá que decidir cuál seguir.
  - Item Type, Creators, Date Added y Date Modified son propiedades del ítem, no filas de campos.
  - attachment, note y annotation no son tipos bibliográficos: probablemente no hacen falta en la biblioteca.
